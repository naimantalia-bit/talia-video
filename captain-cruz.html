Captain Cruz

A screensaver for the Ripples printing machines in Disney Cruise Line's special cafés on board, built to make the guest experience feel bigger.

Client
Disney Cruise Line, through Ripples
Deliverable
Looping screensaver for the printing machine
My role
Art direction, photo compositing, animation, edit
Tools
Photoshop, Kling, Premiere

The film runs as a loop on the machine, exported as a GIF.

The brief

An atmospheric screen that belongs in the attraction

Disney Cruise Line uses the Ripples machine to print on coffee in its special cafés on the ships. Two things make the attraction: a selfie of the guest, and a print on the cup. The screen above the machine had to add to that moment, not compete with it.

The brief was atmosphere that fits the surroundings. So the background is a real ship, and so is the character who greets the guests.

The idea

Let Captain Mickey show what guests get

Captain Mickey is an original puppet who welcomes guests on board. I let him demonstrate the experience himself: he receives a selfie of himself, gets excited, and shows the café's one-of-a-kind print on the cup.

The character explains the product without a single word on screen: a selfie goes in, a print comes out.

He shows guests what makes the ship special, on the ship itself.

The process

Real ship, real puppet, real prints

  1. Illustrations inReceived Disney's original illustrations to work from.
  2. Real printsTurned them into actual prints on coffee, so the cups in the film are real, not rendered.
  3. Photoshop platesTook the background from a real ship and the character from the real puppet. Every frame was worked in Photoshop and the printed cups were placed in.
  4. AnimationAnimated each image in Kling.
  5. EditCut the film in Premiere.
  6. Loop for the machineExported it as a looping GIF, since that is how the machine plays it.

A note on timing

This film was made before Veo and Seedance existed. The animation had to be solved with the tools of that moment, shot by shot, with Photoshop doing the heavy lifting between each frame.
