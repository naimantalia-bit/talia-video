About this game

Talia designed Grandma and forgot the one thing she asked for: a purple hat. Now Grandma is back on a cloud with a bag of hammers, and Talia's village is in the way.

Run seven hand-painted streets, from the art studio to the market. Jump the rolling hammers, time the swinging ones, and keep moving when Grandma starts throwing. Reach the village slot at the end for one last spin.

What you do

  • Dodge three kinds of hammer: rolling, swinging and falling.
  • Pick up a shield to take one hit for free.
  • Find the three purple hats hidden above the rooftops.
  • Collect all three and the final slot spin is rigged in your favor.
  • Listen to Grandma comment on every mistake you make.

What's new

  • Three new streets: the bakery, the greenhouse and the market.
  • Shields and hidden purple hats.
  • Grandma finally gets her hat, if you earn it.
